# Last Days Revival art direction

## Core rule: slot first, material second

Minecraft's Vanilla asset determines the technical slot, model, alpha mask,
animation, and gameplay readability. It does **not** force the Revival texture
to depict the same literal material.

Last Days is a post-apocalyptic reinterpretation of Minecraft. New assets may
become salvaged construction, machinery, containment systems, damaged
architecture, biological growth, waste, or improvised technology when that
creates a stronger world.

## Existing precedent

The sixteen Wool blocks are a coherent family of coloured, bolted industrial
panels rather than literal wool. Their shared construction matters more than
Vanilla material fidelity.

## Design constraints

- Preserve the path, dimensions, alpha/UV layout, and required animation.
- If authored Last Days artwork already exists, migrate it without redesign;
  generation is reserved for genuinely missing visual concepts.
- Keep related Vanilla variants as one coherent Last Days object family.
- Preserve useful colour distinction where players rely on it.
- Prefer readable construction and material logic over surface-only recolours.
- Avoid clean, cute, glossy, or untouched materials.
- Use dirt, soot, corrosion, repairs, bolts, seams, fractures, labels without
  readable text, and uneven wear as recurring visual language.
- A block may be radically reinterpreted, but it must remain recognizable as
  the same inventory/building slot across its family.

## Initial 26.2 reinterpretations

| Minecraft family | Last Days role |
|---|---|
| Shelves | improvised storage built from the matching salvaged wood |
| Cinnabar | contaminated red masonry / reactor-lining mineral |
| Masonry and mineral slots | Moonlab walls, dark fortification scrap, cracked institutional tile, chipped concrete, Madness Material, and hazard storage |
| Sulfur | toxic yellow insulation and chemical shielding |
| Potent sulfur | actively leaking or energized containment material |
| Sulfur spikes | hardened chemical deposits / ruptured insulation growth |
| Resin | hardened amber repair compound and old electrical composite |
| Creaking Heart | biomechanical relay and pressure pump grown into ruined infrastructure |
| Dried Ghast | collapsed biofilter / surveillance aerostat whose membrane reactivates when hydrated |
| Copper utilities | salvaged power-grid fittings progressing from rust-brown copper to deep green patina |
| Pale Oak | ash-bleached cablewood and dead biomechanical utility lattice |
| Pale Garden | fungal acoustic insulation threaded with shuttered optical intrusion sensors |
| Ground flora | leak-detector thickets, wire grass, rust marker buds, and mouldy insulation litter |
| Technical control blocks | bunker module couplings, abandoned construction terminals, and field-diagnostic state panels |
| Wheat growth cycle | legacy Water Purifier filling toward a harvestable Clean Water Dose |

These roles are working canon. They may evolve after in-game visual testing.
